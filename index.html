About Me

I'm a detail-oriented Computer Engineering undergraduate at De La Salle University and a consistent Dean's Lister, recognized for five terms (AY 2024–26). I have hands-on experience in full-stack and software development, backed by a foundation in embedded systems and hardware design.

I led backend and database development for an inventory management system using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, and Docker, and built a YOLOv8 crack detection pipeline with 90.5% accuracy.
